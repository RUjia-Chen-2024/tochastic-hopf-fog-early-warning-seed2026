Primary evaluation used the latest 5-s endpoint checkpoints with 128 paths per origin, a strict 0.80 threshold and confirmation at two consecutive origins. Python, NumPy and PyTorch inference streams were reset to seed 2026 for each record and diffusion condition, without record-specific seed offsets. The same saved paths produced the 1-, 2-, 3- and 5-s event risks. The primary evaluation was exported directly from the 1-s column rather than resimulated. It covered 80/91 onsets within 1~s, issued 146 alarms, and achieved a median lead of 0.525~s and pooled Brier score 0.115940.

With the same fitted checkpoints, suppressing diffusion covered 82/91 onsets, issued 201 alarms and yielded Brier score 0.135900, with median lead 0.835~s. Restoring diffusion reduced alarm burden and probability error, but covered 2 fewer events and shortened warning lead. Thus, unchanged event coverage from earlier random-stream analyses is not claimed for this unified run.

CPU and CUDA RNG states were archived immediately before each simulation batch. Full decoded force paths and per-path first-onset times were retained with SHA256 checksums. Deterministic algorithms were enabled, TF32 was disabled, and the batch size, manifest order and software configuration were fixed. Replaying the archived stream reproduced complete paths exactly in the recorded environment. Main and multihorizon one-second probabilities, labels and alarms were identical.
